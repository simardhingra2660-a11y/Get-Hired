# Get-Hired

**The problem:**
The problem
A focused job search is the same loop every week: check LinkedIn, check the job sites for each country, check the careers pages of companies you like, read each posting, judge the fit, and find a person to contact. The best roles are often not on LinkedIn at all. They sit on a startup's Ashby or Greenhouse board, on a VC fund's portfolio job board, or in a "we're hiring" post. Covering all of that by hand for several locations takes hours, so most people only check one or two sources.

I built an agent that covers those sources in one run, filters out stale and off-target postings with code, and leaves me with a short list to approve.
